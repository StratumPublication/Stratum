About

We publish the evidence, including when it's weak

Skincare has an incentive problem. The people with the most reach usually have something to sell, and a claim propped up by one small company-funded study reads exactly like one backed by a decade of proper trials.

Stratum exists to keep those two apart. Every claim is graded, sourced and dated, and we rewrite pieces when the research moves. We also try to write like humans, because an explanation nobody finishes reading isn't much use to anyone.

Who writes this

Navean. I am not a doctor, a dermatologist or a cosmetic chemist. I am a researcher who got tired of skincare claims that nobody could trace back to anything, and started reading the papers instead.

That limitation is the reason this site is built the way it is. I can’t ask you to trust my judgement, so I don’t: every claim carries its study, its grade, and a link straight to PubMed or the DOI so you can check my work without taking my word for any of it. Where I have got something wrong, I say so in the revision log at the bottom of the article rather than quietly editing it.

No article here has yet been reviewed by a clinician. Every article says so at the top. When that changes, the byline will name who reviewed it and what they are qualified in.
